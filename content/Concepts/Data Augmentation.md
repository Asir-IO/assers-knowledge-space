## What it is
You've got, say an animal, dataset and the "*cat*" class is under-represented in it...   
You've got plenty of examples showing what a dog, a frog, and a rabbit is, but only a *single* example showing what a cat is.   
![[Data Augmentation 2026-03-29 21.25.12.excalidraw]]

Is there anything u could do?   
Sure there is, synthesize new examples for cats by milking your knowledge about what a "*cat*" is.
