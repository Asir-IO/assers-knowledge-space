## Mental Model
A NN can be thought of as an object that, <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">builds</mark> squiggles and sequentially combines them into higher level ones, until finally <mark style="background: #D2B3FFA6;">processing</mark> the final set of squiggles into surfaces that correctly fit the model's training examples.
